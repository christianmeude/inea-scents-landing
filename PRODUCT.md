# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
- **Leads / Prospective Customers**: People discovering the Inea Scents perfume bar service, likely arriving via social media ads or organic search.

## Product Purpose
A lead-generating landing page. Success means high conversion rates—turning visitors into interested prospects who either book immediately (via deep links to the mobile app or web booking) or leave their contact details for a bespoke event consultation.

## Positioning
Offers quality products and excellent service. It exudes elegance and grace, while being generous with the packages and services provided. The page must instantly communicate high-end, premium service.

## Operating Context
A single-page or simple multi-page marketing site. Visitors are evaluating whether this service is right for their wedding, corporate event, or party. The mode is heavily "Persuade".

## Capabilities and Constraints
Must be highly performant, accessible, and SEO-optimized. It must work flawlessly across all device sizes (responsive web). Loading times must be near instant.

## Brand Commitments
- Core aesthetic values: Elegance and grace
- Existing Facebook business page: https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61580331093927

## Product Principles
- **Persuasion & Storytelling**: Use bold typography, high-quality imagery, and clear value propositions to convince visitors of the brand's premium quality.
- **Clear Call-to-Action**: The next step for a visitor (e.g., "Book Your Event", "Inquire Now") must always be obvious and highly tactile.
- **Performance is Polish**: The site must load quickly and animate smoothly. Jitter or lag breaks the illusion of premium quality.
